# A real recipe, from the game's own data, made by the survivor.
#
# One fur into one leather takes 36,000 moves, which is 360 turns at a hundred a turn. The scenario gives it 300, checks
# that nothing has appeared yet, then gives it a hundred more and checks that the leather is on the floor. A craft that
# finished early would fail the first check; one that never finished would fail the last.
#
# The survivor is wearing nothing, so the leather has nowhere to be put and lands on the floor - which is
# `i_add_or_drop`'s fallback, and the reason `expect carried` here is zero rather than one.
world 12 bare
survivor 0 0
items
seed 7
scent 6 6
give fur 1
craft leather 300
expect floor leather 0
craft leather 100
expect floor leather 1
expect carried leather 0
